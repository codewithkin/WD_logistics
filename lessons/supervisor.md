# WD Logistics — Guide for Supervisors

You run day-to-day operations: fleet, trips, customers, suppliers, and finance entries. You can **create** records directly. **Changing or deleting** one goes to an admin as a request — you fill in the edit as normal, say why, and they accept or refuse it. The things kept apart from you are revenue and profit figures, reports, paying money *into* an account, and user management.

## What you can do

- Add trucks, drivers, trailers, customers, suppliers, trips, expenses, invoices and payments — immediately live.
- Edit or delete any of those by sending the change to an admin for approval.
- Move a trip's status along — scheduled, in progress, completed — **directly**, with no approval. That is the one exception, because it is what operations does all day from a yard.
- See what things cost: expense amounts, the breakdown per truck, and what is in each account.
- Take money **out** of an account, and record what you spend.
- View and manage inventory allocations.

## What you can't do

- Delete or change a record on your own — it becomes a request instead.
- See revenue, profit or margin anywhere, or open any report.
- Pay money **into** an account, or set a starting balance.
- Manage users or organisation settings.
- Approve someone else's edit request. That is the admin's job, including your own requests.

## Key actions

### 1. Add a truck or driver

1. Go to **Fleet → Trucks** (or **Drivers**) → **New**.
2. Fill in the details and save — a brand new record is immediately live, with no approval step.

### 2. Create and manage a trip

1. **Operations → Trips → New Trip.**
2. Assign the truck, driver, customer, and route.
3. As the trip progresses, come back and update its status (in progress, completed) — this is what feeds the reports admins see later.

### 3. Record expenses and payments

Same flow as staff — **Operations → Expenses** and **Finance → Customer Payments**. Recording a new one is direct. If something was entered wrong, open it, correct it and give a reason: an admin sees the before and after and accepts or refuses it.

### 4. Send a change for approval

1. Open the record and press **Edit**, exactly as if you were changing it.
2. Make the change and give a short reason — that is what the admin reads.
3. You will see "sent to an admin for approval". The record does not change until they accept it, and the page shows a pending badge meanwhile.
4. Only an admin can approve, including on your own requests. Ask one to look at the queue if it is urgent.

### 5. Manage customers and suppliers

**Customers** and **Suppliers** in the sidebar work the same way — add them directly, track balances (what a customer owes you, or what you owe a supplier), and send any change to an admin for approval.

## Why reports and user management aren't here

The rule behind all of it: **you may see what things cost, but not what the business earns.** Costs are operational — you need them to do the job. Revenue, profit and margin are the owner's business, which is why reports are admin-only too. Everything else that keeps the fleet running day to day is yours.
