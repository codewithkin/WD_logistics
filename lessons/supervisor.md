# WD Logistics — Guide for Supervisors

You run day-to-day operations: fleet, trips, customers, suppliers, and finance entries. You can create and edit records directly — no approval needed. The two things kept apart from you are financial reports and account/user management, which stay with admins.

## What you can do

- Full add/edit access to trucks, drivers, customers, suppliers, trips, expenses, invoices, and payments.
- Review and approve (or reject) staff Edit Requests.
- View and manage inventory allocations.

## What you can't do

- Delete records (only admins can — this is a safety net, not a trust issue).
- View revenue, profit, or performance reports.
- Manage users or organization settings.

## Key actions

### 1. Add a truck or driver

1. Go to **Fleet → Trucks** (or **Drivers**) → **New**.
2. Fill in the details and save — it's immediately live, no approval step for you.

### 2. Create and manage a trip

1. **Operations → Trips → New Trip.**
2. Assign the truck, driver, customer, and route.
3. As the trip progresses, come back and update its status (in progress, completed) — this is what feeds the reports admins see later.

### 3. Record expenses and payments

Same flow as staff — **Operations → Expenses** and **Finance → Customer Payments** — except you can also edit or void an entry afterward if something was entered wrong, without submitting a request.

### 4. Review a staff Edit Request

1. Go to **Edit Requests** in the sidebar — pending ones show a badge.
2. Open one to see the original data side-by-side with what's being requested, and the reason given.
3. **Approve** to apply the change immediately, or **Reject** with a note explaining why.

### 5. Manage customers and suppliers

**Customers** and **Suppliers** in the sidebar work the same way — add, edit, and track balances (what a customer owes you, or what you owe a supplier) directly.

## Why reports and user management aren't here

Those two are kept admin-only by design — reports carry sensitive revenue/profit numbers, and user management controls who can log in at all. Everything else that keeps the fleet running day-to-day is yours to manage directly.
